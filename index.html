Why ocean currents

Renewable power without the gaps.

Sun sets, wind drops, tides turn. Deep ocean currents flow in one direction, day and night — so the energy they carry needs no battery bank to back it up.

Technology

No blades. Just a controlled vortex.

Current enters an enclosed housing, where a proprietary geometry turns straight-line flow into rotation. There are no exposed blades — the rotating core sits inside the structure.

Swipe the diagram sideways →

01 / Intake

Ocean current enters

A shaped inlet captures the steady deep-sea flow and accelerates it into the chamber — no dependency on tides or waves.

02 / Induction

Vortex generation

Proprietary bladeless vortex induction converts linear flow into efficient rotation inside the enclosed core.

03 / Conversion

Direct-drive generator

Rotation drives the generator directly, without a gearbox, and power is exported to shore by subsea cable.

Partnership model

No CAPEX.No OPEX.

We finance, build, operate and maintain every project. You buy the electricity — costs are covered through a monthly energy charge under a long-term power purchase agreement.
